PRIVACY POLICY / UPDATED NOVEMBER 15, 2026

Your game.
Your information.

This policy covers Chess: Time Wars on iPhone, iPad and Android, and this information and support website.

Data practices differ by platform. The Apple services and advertising sections apply to the Apple app. See the Android privacy section for the offline game, purchases and ads, and the section on optional Android online matches for Google sign-in and the match service.

Questions or requests: supportchesstimewars@gmail.com.

Apple app: game data on your device

The app stores preferences, unfinished computer matches, turn history and local win/loss records on your device to run the game and let you resume play. Computer move calculations run on your device. The app does not require a separate Chess: Time Wars account or operate a developer-hosted gameplay database. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings.

Apple services

Online multiplayer uses Apple Game Center. Apple processes account and matchmaking information; game-session information is exchanged between participating players so the match can run. Your Game Center identity may be visible to your opponent. Apple also processes in-app payments, purchase records, restores and refund requests through StoreKit. All game modes are free. The app receives verified purchase information to restore ad removal, recognize previous Complete Pack ad-removal benefits and process optional repeatable coffee tips; it does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Apple services may process diagnostic information under their applicable terms. Please review Apple's privacy policy and your Apple privacy and Game Center settings for these services.

Apple app: advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for ads and applicable consent choices. The public App Store version can show revenue-generating full-screen ads at eligible completed-match breaks for players without verified ad removal. Active games and tutorials are ad-free. Advertising contacts Google's services.

Banners may appear on Home, in the pre-match lobby and on completed-match Results, not on an active board or tutorial. Each whole completed match may offer one ready full-screen ad at the return-to-Start or rematch break, with a minimum three-minute gap between full-screen ads. Partial Infinity results, ordinary launch/resume and reality changes are not full-screen ad opportunities. Missing or ineligible ads are skipped without waiting; a later load does not interrupt play.

Google's SDK may process IP addresses, device and app information, diagnostics, identifiers and ad interactions to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. IP addresses can be used to estimate general location. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier, and does not request Apple's tracking permission. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed.

Required consent is checked before requesting ads. Where applicable, revisit Options > Ad Privacy Choices to change your choices. Remove Ads or previous Complete Pack ownership stops new requests and presentations for both banners and full-screen ads after ownership is verified; it does not delete data already processed by a provider. Read Google's privacy policy and Google's SDK data disclosure for more information.

Android: offline play, purchases and ads

Chess: Time Wars computer play on Android does not require a separate game account. Computer opponents run on your device. Saved matches, turn history, tutorials, preferences, age-group selection and personal results stay in the app's private local storage. The app does not send saved computer games or their results to the match service. Android application backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes those local records; support cannot recover them remotely. Google Play and Android may process their own installation, account and diagnostic information.

Every ruleset, Infinity, difficulty and tutorial is free. Google Play purchases and Google AdMob advertising are available only for players who select the adult age group, subject to applicable privacy choices. Younger players and players who have not selected an age group can play offline without app-initiated requests for these services. The age selection is not proof of age or verified parental consent. An optional purchase is never required to play, and does not sign you in to online matches.

Google Play Purchases

For players who select the adult age group, the app may ask Google Play for current product prices and ownership. It receives product IDs, purchase state, signed receipts and purchase tokens. It uses this information locally to verify ownership, restore ad-removal benefits and acknowledge durable purchases with Google. Signed ad-removal receipts may be retained in private, non-backed-up local storage for offline verification. Coffee tips are verified and consumed with Google so they can be repeated; consumed tips do not become permanent ad-removal ownership or restorable entitlements. The app does not send receipts to an advertising network or a developer-hosted purchase database. No account name/email or obfuscated game-account identifier is attached by the game.

Checking ownership does not make a purchase. You must choose an item and confirm any charge through Google Play. Google manages payment and transaction records under its own policies; the game does not receive your card details, bank details or Google password.

Google AdMob and Privacy Choices

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable regional privacy choices before ad requests. For eligible adults, banners may appear on Home, in the pre-match lobby and on completed-match Results. One ready full-screen ad may appear when you return to Start or choose a rematch after a completed whole match, with at least three minutes between full-screen ads. No ads interrupt active games, tutorials or partial Infinity results. Missing or failed ads do not delay play. Google controls the close button.

Google's SDK processing can include IP-based general location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device/other identifiers. It supports advertising, measurement and abuse/security checks. Requests use non-personalized settings, G-rated/child-directed treatment and disabled publisher first-party IDs. The app removes Android advertising-ID and ad-services permissions. Those restrictions do not mean that no information is processed. SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Where offered, revisit Advertising Privacy Choices in Options > Store & Restore Purchases. Verified Remove Ads or previous Complete Pack ownership suppresses new requests/presentations for both ad formats; unknown Play ownership fails closed. Changing your advertising choices does not automatically erase previously processed Google data. Review https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's account/ad privacy controls for provider-held information. Google determines applicable provider retention; the game does not promise a specific Google deletion deadline.

Contact supportchesstimewars@gmail.com or use https://chesstimewars.github.io/account-deletion.html to ask about data under our control without reinstalling. We can handle support correspondence and explain local-record removal and Google's payment/advertising privacy routes. An email request is not an automatic confirmation that provider records were erased. Do not send passwords, verification codes, card numbers, identity documents or children's personal details. Optional Android Online Matches

Computer games and tutorials do not require an account. Optional online play is available only to adults who choose their age group, accept the online notice and explicitly sign in through Google's account chooser. Younger and unselected age groups remain offline; no parental-verification service is active. Age selection is self-declared, not independently verified. The app does not ask for a child's name, birth date, school, contacts or guardian documents.

Online play creates a game identity with Firebase Authentication using Google sign-in. Google handles account credentials; do not send us a Google password. Firebase may process your Google account identifier, email and provider profile information supplied during sign-in, together with IP address and authentication / security information. The match service receives a game user ID and verified session information, not a public email/name/profile. Google/Firebase's own privacy and retention practices also apply.

Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check verify that online requests come from a recognized, licensed Play installation. Google processes app, installation and device/security signals for this verification. It is not advertising or an identity/age-verification service. No debug attestation bypass is enabled for the hosted game.

The service stores game membership, moves, current and historical positions, time-travel orders, turn deadlines, results and eligibility/security records so it can enforce the rules, reconnect players and prevent unauthorized access. Your opponent receives only the game information they are allowed to see; pending opposing destinations are not disclosed. There is no public profile, free-text chat, contacts upload or player directory. A friend code can be shared through an app you choose; that app's privacy practices apply to the share.

You can invite a friend or ask Time Wars to find another online player using the same rules and time-travel settings. For automatic matching, the service processes your private game-account identifier, selected rules, a random search-request identifier and search timing/status. These queue records are not a public player directory and are not available for other players to browse. No contact upload, public profile, free-text chat or location-based matching is used.

You can cancel a search. Searches stop after two minutes, or expire shortly after the app stops checking while in the background. If an opponent connected before cancellation was confirmed, the app recovers that game. Search-request receipts are scheduled for deletion after seven days so retries and lost responses do not create duplicate games. Starting another search does not extend an older receipt's retention period. Account deletion removes the account's queue records and ends unfinished online games as explained below.

Online communications use encrypted connections. Active online games are ad-free; ad placements are separate from the active board. Disabling optional online play does not delete data already processed by Google or the match service.

Completed whole matches are scheduled for deletion after seven days. A completed board inside an ongoing Infinity match is not a completed whole match. Unstarted invitations expire after 24 hours and are scheduled for cleanup. Active matches remain until completed, resigned, timed out or affected by an account deletion. Cleanup is an operational process, not a promise of erasure at an exact second.

Google Cloud's request and operational logs may contain IP addresses, request paths, timestamps, response codes and device/network client information. The project's default operational-log retention is 30 days. Google Cloud's required administrative/security audit-log bucket has 400-day retention; those logs primarily describe administration of the service. Google may retain additional provider security records under its own policies. Game code does not log raw sign-in tokens, deletion secrets, account profile data or private board states. Signing out or deleting a game account does not automatically erase provider logs that remain subject to these separate retention practices.

The current online lobby offers Delete Online Account, with a confirmation before any deletion request. Deletion ends unfinished matches as a resignation and removes their game data; the other player can receive a short private account-deletion notice. It does not erase a result already stored locally on another device or delete your underlying Google account or offline saves. Provider deletion failures remain denied and queued for retry rather than silently restoring online access. Necessary security/deletion-status records have separately reviewed retention limits.

Private deletion-status receipts and opponent deletion notices are scheduled for removal after seven days. Revoked/disabled account eligibility and Firebase account records remain until an account-deletion request is completed; revoking access is not the same as erasing the account. A private deletion ownership key can be used through the secure external process even if sign-in is disabled.

You can also request deletion without reinstalling at Account & Data Deletion or contact supportchesstimewars@gmail.com. Ownership must be verified; an invitation code or an unverified email is not enough to erase an account. A private deletion key is a secret, not an invitation: never post or email it, passwords, verification codes or identity documents. Use only the approved secure deletion process. Existing support targets remain acknowledgment within 24 hours and resolution within 15 days; provider/network delays should be explained rather than hidden.

If you email support, the message, your email address and attachments are processed through Gmail and used to answer your question, investigate problems and maintain a record of the request. Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until removed by the app or by deleting the app. Apple app backups are managed separately in your device/account settings; Android application backup is disabled. Purchase and Game Center records for the Apple app are managed by Apple, and its advertising data by Google, under their policies. You can request access to or deletion of support correspondence under our control from the Account & Data Deletion page, without reinstalling the app. Any information that must be retained for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations may need to be retained for those purposes.

Withdrawing ad consent or deleting local app data does not automatically erase records held by Apple or Google. Use their account/privacy controls for those requests. Your rights can vary depending on where you live.

Children and Platform Differences

The age rating describes game content; it is not proof of a player's age or parental consent to data processing. India's DPDP framework defines a child as a person under 18. Its provisions have phased commencement dates, and child privacy protections must be considered separately from a store content rating.

Apple app. There is no separate Chess: Time Wars child account, in-game age verification or guardian-approval service in the current Apple app. Game Center, StoreKit and Google's advertising/privacy SDKs can process information as described in this policy. Apple's parental and purchase controls may be useful, but we do not represent them as an in-game verified guardian-consent service or as disabling every third-party SDK request for a child.

Android app. Computer play and tutorials are account-free. The app does not start optional advertising, purchase or online-play requests for younger players or players whose age group is unknown. Advertising and purchases require a self-declared age group of 18 or older; advertising is also subject to applicable privacy choices. Optional online play separately requires the online notice and explicit Google sign-in. This is an age-group safeguard, not identity or age verification. There is no verified parental-consent service or child online account.

A parent or guardian with a concern can contact support without including a child's details in the first message. We will explain the appropriate next steps and address information under our control. Do not send a child's personal information in a bug report.
