PRIVACY POLICY / UPDATED OCTOBER 16, 2026

Your game.
Your information.

This policy covers the Chess: Time Wars Apple app, the Android beta, optional commerce-test build and optional online beta, and this information and support website.

Data practices differ by platform. The Apple services and advertising sections below apply only to the Apple app. See the Android beta section for the offline game and optional Android commerce tests, or optional online friend matches.

Questions or requests: supportchesstimewars@gmail.com.

Apple app: game data on your device

The app stores preferences, unfinished computer matches, turn history and local win/loss records on your device to run the game and let you resume play. Computer move calculations run on your device. The app does not require a separate Chess: Time Wars account or operate a developer-hosted gameplay database. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings.

Apple services

Online multiplayer uses Apple Game Center. Apple processes account and matchmaking information; game-session information is exchanged between participating players so the match can run. Your Game Center identity may be visible to your opponent. Apple also processes in-app payments, purchase records, restores and refund requests through StoreKit. The app receives verified purchase information to enable owned features; it does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Apple services may process diagnostic information under their applicable terms. Please review Apple's privacy policy and your Apple privacy and Game Center settings for these services.

Apple app: advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for full-screen ads between matches and applicable consent choices. The public App Store version can show revenue-generating ads for players who have not purchased ad removal. Ads appear only at eligible between-match breaks. Advertising contacts Google's services.

Google's SDK may process IP addresses, device and app information, diagnostics, identifiers and ad interactions to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. IP addresses can be used to estimate general location. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier, and does not request Apple's tracking permission. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed.

Required consent is checked before requesting ads. Where applicable, revisit Options > Ad Privacy Choices to change your choices. Remove Ads or the Complete Pack stops new ad requests and presentations after ownership is verified; it does not delete data already processed by a provider. Read Google's privacy policy and Google's SDK data disclosure for more information.

Android: offline play and optional commerce tests

Chess: Time Wars on Android does not require a separate game account. Computer opponents run on your device. Saved matches, turn history, tutorials, preferences, age-group/test choices and personal results stay in the app's private local storage. Android application backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes those local records; support cannot recover them remotely.

The offline beta, including 0.3-beta, has no ads, purchases or live multiplayer and does not request Internet permission. It does not send saved games or match results to a developer-hosted service. Google Play and Android can process their own installation/account/diagnostic data under Google's policies.

The optional commerce-test build is identified by Store & Restore Purchases in Options. Every beta ruleset remains unlocked. Labelled free purchase previews are local simulations, not transactions with Google. This build adds Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Optional network tests are off initially. They require a neutral adult tester selection and a separate explanation/enable action for purchases or advertising. Declining either does not prevent offline play or enable the other service. Younger and unknown-age testers remain offline for these optional tests. This is a conservative beta safeguard, not proof of age or a parental-verification service.

Google Play Purchase Testing

After purchase testing is enabled, the app asks Google Play for current product prices and ownership and receives product IDs, purchase state, signed receipts and purchase tokens. It uses this information locally to check ownership, restore benefits and acknowledge completed purchases with Google. It does not send receipts to an advertising network or a developer-hosted purchase database. No account name/email or obfuscated game-account identifier is attached by the game. Google manages payment and transaction records under its own policies; the game does not receive card details, bank details or your Google password.

Opening Google checkout requires another explicit action. Only approved Google license testers should attempt purchase tests, using Google's test payment methods. Beta enrollment alone does not make a transaction free: cancel if Google offers a real charge rather than a test purchase. Turning purchase tests off stops new game-initiated Play requests; it does not cancel/refund a purchase or remove Google's existing transaction records. Local previews do not grant or erase real Google ownership.

Google Test Advertising And Privacy Choices

After advertising is explicitly enabled, Google's privacy SDK checks applicable regional choices before any ad request. The app uses only Google's sample full-screen interstitial ad unit for this beta. The app identity registered with AdMob lets it retrieve the Android privacy message; that identity is not a live revenue ad unit. No banner covers/resizes the chess board. Eligible ad breaks occur only after completed whole matches, never during a move, a tutorial or a single Infinity board's result. Missing/failed ads do not delay play. Google controls the close button.

Google's SDK processing can include IP-based general location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device/other identifiers. It supports advertising, measurement and abuse/security checks. Requests use non-personalized settings, G-rated/child-directed treatment and disabled publisher first-party IDs. The app removes Android advertising-ID and ad-services permissions. Those restrictions do not mean that no information is processed. SDK data is encrypted in transit.

Advertising can be disabled in the store controls. Required Advertising Privacy Choices remain available after a prior consent check, including when advertising is turned off. Verified Remove Ads/Complete Pack ownership suppresses new ad requests/presentations; unknown Play ownership fails closed. Disabling advertising or withdrawing choices does not automatically erase previously processed Google data. Review https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google's account/ad privacy controls for provider-held information. Google determines applicable provider retention; the game does not promise a specific Google deletion deadline.

Support, Deletion And Children

Contact supportchesstimewars@gmail.com or use https://chesstimewars.github.io/account-deletion.html to ask about data under our control without reinstalling. We can handle support correspondence and explain local-record removal and Google's payment/advertising privacy routes. An email request is not an automatic confirmation that provider records were erased. Do not send passwords, verification codes, card numbers, identity documents or children's personal details. A parent/guardian may contact support without including a child's details in the first message. No child account or guardian consent intake is introduced by this beta.

Android live multiplayer, online accounts and public guardian verification are not part of this build. Those features require another policy/declaration review before being distributed. The Apple version continues to use its separately described Apple services and advertising policy.

Optional Android Online Friend Matches

Computer games and tutorials do not require an account. The initial online beta is available only to adults who choose their age group, accept the online notice and explicitly use Google's account chooser. Younger and unselected age groups remain offline; no parental-verification service is active. Age selection is self-declared, not independently verified. The app does not ask for a child's name, birth date, school, contacts or guardian documents.

Online play creates a game identity with Firebase Authentication using Google sign-in. Google handles account credentials; do not send us a Google password. Firebase may process your Google account identifier, email and provider profile information supplied during sign-in, together with IP address and authentication / security information. The match service receives a game user ID and verified session information, not a public email/name/profile. Google/Firebase's own privacy and retention practices also apply.

Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check verify that online requests come from a recognized, licensed Play installation. Google processes app, installation and device/security signals for this verification. It is not advertising or an identity/age-verification service. No debug attestation bypass is enabled for the hosted game.

The service stores game membership, moves, current and historical positions, time-travel orders, turn deadlines, results and eligibility/security records so it can enforce the rules, reconnect players and prevent unauthorized access. Your opponent receives only the game information they are allowed to see; pending opposing destinations are not disclosed. There is no public profile, free-text chat, contacts upload or player directory. A friend code can be shared through an app you choose; that app's privacy practices apply to the share.

Online communications use encrypted connections. The online beta has no ads, purchase checkout or Analytics SDK. Separate commerce-test versions retain their own disclosed optional advertising and purchase behavior. Disabling optional online play does not delete data already processed by Google or the game server.

Completed whole matches are scheduled for deletion after seven days. A completed board inside an ongoing Infinity match is not a completed whole match. Unstarted invitations expire after 24 hours and are scheduled for cleanup. Active matches remain until completed, resigned, timed out or affected by an account deletion. Cleanup is an operational process, not a promise of erasure at an exact second.

Google Cloud's request and operational logs may contain IP addresses, request paths, timestamps, response codes and device/network client information. The project's default operational-log retention is 30 days. Google Cloud's required administrative/security audit-log bucket has 400-day retention; those logs primarily describe administration of the service. Google may retain additional provider security records under its own policies. Game code does not log raw sign-in tokens, deletion secrets, account profile data or private board states. Signing out or deleting a game account does not automatically erase provider logs that remain subject to these separate retention practices.

You can sign out, disable online access or request account deletion through the online lobby. Deletion ends unfinished matches as a resignation and removes their game data; the other player can receive a short private account-deletion notice. It does not erase a result already stored locally on another device or delete your underlying Google account. Provider deletion failures remain denied and queued for retry rather than silently restoring online access. Necessary security/deletion-status records have separately reviewed retention limits.

Private deletion-status receipts and opponent deletion notices are scheduled for removal after seven days. Revoked/disabled account eligibility and Firebase account records remain until an account-deletion request is completed; revoking access is not the same as erasing the account. A private deletion ownership key can be used through the secure external process even if sign-in is disabled.

You can also request deletion without reinstalling at https://chesstimewars.github.io/account-deletion.html or contact supportchesstimewars@gmail.com. Ownership must be verified; an invitation code or an unverified email is not enough to erase an account. A private deletion key is a secret, not an invitation: never post or email it, passwords, verification codes or identity documents. Use only the approved secure deletion process. Existing support targets remain acknowledgment within 24 hours and resolution within 15 days; provider/network delays should be explained rather than hidden.

If you email support, the message, your email address and attachments are processed through Gmail and used to answer your question, investigate problems and maintain a record of the request. Send only information relevant to the issue. We do not ask for passwords, verification codes or card details, and do not use support email as an automatic marketing subscription.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until removed by the app or by deleting the app. Apple app backups are managed separately in your device/account settings; Android application backup is disabled in the beta. Purchase and Game Center records for the Apple app are managed by Apple, and its advertising data by Google, under their policies. You can request access to or deletion of support correspondence under our control from the Account & Data Deletion page, without reinstalling the app. Any information that must be retained for security, dispute resolution or legal obligations may need to be retained for those purposes.

Withdrawing ad consent or deleting local app data does not automatically erase records held by Apple or Google. Use their account/privacy controls for those requests. Your rights can vary depending on where you live.

Children and Platform Differences

The age rating describes game content; it is not proof of a player's age or parental consent to data processing. India's DPDP framework defines a child as a person under 18. Its provisions have phased commencement dates, and child privacy protections must be considered separately from a store content rating.

Apple app. There is no separate Chess: Time Wars child account, in-game age verification or guardian-approval service in the current Apple app. Game Center, StoreKit and Google's advertising/privacy SDKs can process information as described in this policy. Apple's parental and purchase controls may be useful, but we do not represent them as an in-game verified guardian-consent service or as disabling every third-party SDK request for a child.

Android offline beta. Computer play is account-free, with saved games and results kept on the device. The offline beta has no advertising, purchases or live multiplayer and does not request Internet permission.

Android optional commerce-test build. Younger and unknown-age testers cannot enable its optional network tests. A self-declared age of 18 or older and a separate enable action are required for Google Play purchase tests or Google test advertising. This is a beta safeguard, not verified age or parental consent. It does not make a purchase free unless Google license-test checkout is actually being used.

The optional Android online beta requires a self-declared adult age group, the online notice and explicit Google sign-in. Younger and unknown-age players remain offline. No verified parental-consent service or child online account is offered. Online beta availability is separate from offline and commerce-test access; see the online section above for the service data involved.

A parent or guardian with a concern can contact support without including a child's details in the first message. We will explain the appropriate next steps and address information under our control. Do not send a child's personal information in a bug report.
