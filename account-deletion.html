Your data.
Your choice.

Request account or data deletion for Chess: Time Wars, even if you cannot open the app. You do not need to reinstall it to contact us.

Request Deletion by Email

Or email supportchesstimewars@gmail.com with the subject Chess: Time Wars — Account and Data Deletion.

What to include

Tell us whether your request concerns Android, Apple services, or support correspondence, and what you want deleted. Include the app version if you know it. We will explain the applicable steps and any ownership verification before deleting account data. An email address, player ID or friend invitation alone is not permission to erase someone else's account.

Do not email passwords, verification codes, private deletion keys, identity documents, card details, or a child's name, birthday, photo or other personal details. A parent or guardian can contact support without sending those details in the first message.

Android Local Game Data

Android computer play is offline and account-free, including in the optional commerce-test build. Offline and commerce-test play do not create a Chess: Time Wars online account or server match history. Optional online friend matches are described separately below. Matches, settings, tutorials and personal results stay on your device; clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes those local records. Android application backup is disabled in this beta.

To remove local records on Android, open the device's Settings, find Chess: Time Wars under Apps, and select its storage-clearing option. The wording varies by phone. This permanently removes local games and settings; support cannot recover them or erase them remotely.

Optional Android Purchase and Advertising Tests

The commerce-test build can separately enable Google Play purchase testing and Google test advertising for adult testers. Turning those tests off stops new app-initiated requests; it does not erase records already held by Google or cancel/refund a purchase. Clearing app storage removes local preview/test preferences, not Google Play ownership or transaction records.

Use Google Account Data & Privacy and Google's purchase/advertising privacy controls for provider-held information. We can explain the route and handle support correspondence or other data under our control, but cannot erase your Google account, provider logs or Google Play receipts for you. Emailing us does not automatically confirm their deletion. See the Android commerce privacy section for the data involved.

Optional Android Online Accounts

Adult players who explicitly choose Google sign-in for the optional online beta create a Firebase game identity. Younger and unknown-age players remain offline; no public guardian-consent intake is active. Leaving the online screen or uninstalling the app is not account deletion.

Use Delete Online Account in the online lobby and confirm the request, or contact support from the request button above without reinstalling. We require ownership verification, not merely an email address, player ID or friend code. If you retained a private deletion ownership key from an earlier build, the secure deletion tool below can verify ownership without signing in. The current lobby no longer generates these keys. Never email or post an existing key.

Deletion ends unfinished online matches as a resignation and removes the game account and associated server match data. Another player can receive a private deletion notice; results already saved on their device remain unchanged. A completed board inside an ongoing Infinity match is not a completed whole match. Provider failures remain denied and queued for retry; a pending response is not a completed deletion.

Account deletion also removes private automatic-matchmaking search records and waiting slots. Without account deletion, search-request receipts are scheduled for cleanup after seven days; starting another search does not extend an older receipt's retention.

Completed whole matches, deletion notices and completed private status receipts are scheduled for cleanup after seven days. Account deletion does not delete the underlying Google account, Google Play ownership/receipts, or provider operational/security records subject to separate retention. See the online privacy section.

Secure Online Deletion

This tool sends your private ownership key over HTTPS directly to the Time Wars match service only when you press Delete Online Account. It does not sign you in, create an account, use website analytics or store your key in a URL or browser storage. Keep your key private; it authorizes deletion, not game access.

JavaScript and a secure connection are needed for this tool. You can always contact support above instead.

Retain your private status code until deletion is confirmed. It is not a public support reference. A network error does not prove whether a request arrived; use the same private code to check. If no receipt exists, retry with your original ownership key or contact support without sending either secret.

Apple Accounts and Purchases

The Apple app uses Game Center and App Store purchases, not a separate Chess: Time Wars account. Apple manages those account and purchase records. See Apple's Data and Privacy portal for Apple-held data. Deleting a local game does not cancel or refund a purchase. We can help identify the appropriate privacy route, but cannot erase your Apple Account for you.

Support Correspondence

You can request access to or deletion of messages you sent to support. We may need to verify control of the relevant correspondence. We use your request to handle the privacy issue, not to enroll you in marketing.

We will reply with the next steps and confirm what has been completed; sending an email is not an automatic deletion confirmation. Any necessary retention or provider-controlled records will be explained rather than promised away. See the privacy policy for the current platform-specific data practices.

Privacy Requests and Complaints

You may use the support mailbox for an applicable access, correction, deletion, consent-withdrawal request or a complaint, not just account deletion. Tell us whether it concerns the Apple app, Android app or support correspondence. We will explain the relevant steps, necessary verification and what has actually been completed. Our support page describes the complaints and review process.

Deleting local game data does not refund a purchase, remove store ownership or erase provider-held records. We will distinguish information we can address from Apple- or Google-managed records and explain any necessary legal or security retention rather than promise immediate deletion of everything.
