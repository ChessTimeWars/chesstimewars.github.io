Player Support, Privacy and Complaints

For help with Chess: Time Wars on Apple or Android, email supportchesstimewars@gmail.com. You can report a bug, ask about a move, raise a privacy or safety concern, or make a complaint without reinstalling the app or buying an upgrade. The responsible contact is Chess: Time Wars Player Support and Privacy Contact, reached through this same mailbox.

For a bug report, tell us your device model, operating-system version, app version/build, ruleset, difficulty, player color and turn number. Describe what you expected and what happened. A screenshot or short recording can help; please hide unrelated notifications, account details and other players' personal information before sending it. Send only information needed to understand the problem.

Never send passwords, verification codes, card or bank details, identity documents, private deletion keys, or a child's name, birthday, photograph or other personal details. A parent or guardian can make the first contact without providing a child's details.

Making or Following Up on a Complaint

Use the subject Chess: Time Wars - Complaint or Chess: Time Wars - Privacy Request. Tell us which platform and app version you use, a short description of the issue, and the outcome you are seeking. We will acknowledge the request, give it a reference, and explain the next steps.

We aim to acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 calendar days. If an investigation needs more time, we will explain why, provide a progress update and give the next expected response date. Any shorter deadline required by applicable law takes precedence. These service targets do not limit your rights or require you to wait beyond a legal deadline.

If you disagree with the response, reply in the same email thread with the reference and explain what you would like reviewed. A request for further review does not waive your rights or extend an external appeal deadline.

Apple and Google handle their own payment/refund decisions and account/privacy controls. We will explain the relevant route and remain responsible for handling issues concerning the game or information under our control. Contacting us does not automatically delete provider-held records or authorize a refund.

Information for Players in India

The same support and privacy contact is available to players in India. There is no separate support fee. Please say if your request concerns an online match, a purchase, advertising, local game data or support correspondence so we can identify the appropriate process without asking for unnecessary data.

Where India's online-gaming grievance rules apply, a dissatisfied user may appeal to the Online Gaming Authority of India; the rules also address cases where a grievance is not resolved. The government describes the appeal period as 30 days from the provider's decision, with further appeal provisions. See the Ministry's published explanation of the grievance process for official information. Our internal review process does not replace an available statutory remedy or pause its deadline.

The essentials

See each mechanic in motion in our three short gameplay videos.

How do I start learning time travel?

Tap Start, choose a ruleset, then choose Tutorial. Tutorials are free and skippable. Select the highlighted time-direction control and turn amount to see the available moves change. Easy AI play also includes hints and Undo.

Why are the time-travel buttons disabled?

In Charged mode, your battery needs three blocks. Ordinary moves build charge; time travel spends it. Unlimited mode removes the charging requirement. Movement rules, king safety and available history still apply.

Can my opponent see my hidden travelers?

The player-facing travel preview shows only your own future arrivals. Computer opponents are designed to plan from visible information and their own travelers, not read your hidden destinations.

How do turns work across alternate realities?

You owe a move on each required reality before starting your next round. Choose the order of boards that are ready for you; you can answer a move while your opponent still owes moves elsewhere. Swipe to browse, or use Next Board.

In the default Alternate Realities mode, checkmate on any reality decides the match. With Infinity enabled, a checkmated reality is locked and scores a point; play continues until every reality is completed or destroyed. The player with more checkmates wins; a tied score is decided by the final checkmate. Capturing a reality's founding traveler destroys that unfinished reality without awarding a checkmate point. Drawn realities are also removed without a point.

Why can't I castle?

The king and that rook must not have moved, the squares between them must be clear, and the king cannot castle out of, through, or into check. Time travel does not waive king-safety rules.

Accounts, purchases & ads

When can I download Chess: Time Wars?

Coming Autumn 2026, first on iPhone and iPad. Public downloads will be announced here after App Store approval.

Is Chess: Time Wars coming to Android?

Yes—the Android version is in development. Its release date will be announced once it is ready. The Apple version is coming Autumn 2026.

Game Center says I'm not authenticated

Open iPhone Settings > Game Center and sign in, then reopen the game. Online opponents should use compatible app versions and the same ruleset and charge mode. Play vs AI does not require Game Center.

How do I restore a purchase?

From Start, open Options > Store & Restore Purchases, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. One-time purchases do not need to be bought again. If an item remains locked, email support with its name and app build, but no payment details.

Where do I buy upgrades?

Only inside the iOS app, through Apple's purchase sheet. There are no purchases or payment forms on this website. Forward Time and tutorials are free. Optional one-time purchases unlock Causal Collapse, Alternate Realities, or ad removal; the Complete Pack includes all three benefits.

How can I request a refund?

Apple handles payments and refund decisions. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page using the purchasing Apple Account. We cannot issue App Store refunds directly.

How do I manage privacy or request deletion?

Use our Account & Data Deletion page to request help without reinstalling, or see the privacy policy. Where required, ad consent can be revisited from Options > Ad Privacy Choices. Contact the support email for questions about support correspondence. Apple and Google manage data within their own services.
